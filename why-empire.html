Empire Square Group provides exceptional direct real estate opportunities for select investors using a customized, turnkey solution which achieves outstanding returns. Over the past five years we have earned our members an average 10% annual cash yield and a 20% IRR.

01

Direct Real Estate Investment

We enable our members to invest directly in real estate which ensures they have maximum control over their investments and cuts out middleman costs.

Greater Control

Direct real estate ownership provides a significantly greater amount of control compared to other real estate investment options. We offer our members different levels of control based upon their needs, from 100% control and ownership to minority interests.

Lower Costs

Investing directly in real estate lowers fees and tax costs. Direct investment cuts out the middlemen found in typical REITs, funds, and other real estate investment structures as well as eliminating layers of fees.

Improved Estate Planning

Direct real estate investments can be held by a family long term and handed down through generations. Intergenerational wealth transfer remains one of the most important considerations facing our investing members.

02

Superior Performance

Our proprietary value-oriented strategy has delivered our investors outstanding returns of 10% annual cash yield and 21% IRR across over 28 investments totaling over $500 million dollars in value over the last five years.

Quality Real Estate

We invest in US cities and metropolitan areas which we expect will out-perform over the long run based upon our proprietary analysis. We invest in markets with strong demographic trends, growing educated workforces, a backbone of educational institutions and high quality of life.

Value Focus

We use a fundamental analysis framework for underwriting investments and are relentlessly value-oriented. Our data-driven approach to identifying exceptional income producing real estate assets synthesizes and analyzes information from a multitude of sources to alert us to regions, cities, markets, asset classes, and assets that are poised to outperform. We identify and exploit both macroeconomic and microeconomic dislocations to acquire assets below intrinsic value as assessed using comparable sales, replacement cost, and cash flow generation metrics.

Unique Access

Our ability to access exceptional direct real estate opportunities comes from a proprietary network of long standing relationships with leading real estate owners, brokers, lenders, and operating partners. We also leverage the Empire Square Group member network for opportunities and have built a reputation in the national market for the highest integrity, reliability, and efficiency in closing transactions. We review enormous numbers of opportunities and select only the most compelling opportunities to show to our members based upon their profiles.

Preferential Debt

Our members also enjoy access to debt capital on preferential terms which allows us to optimize cash yield. We have key relationships with leading lender institutions who are able to offer debt financing for acquisitions on terms simply not available to other real estate investors.

Seasoned Team

Our underwriting team, based in New York, brings years of experience and a rigorous institutional approach to underwriting, vetting, diligencing, analyzing and executing on every deal. We leverage the impressive local experience of our select, best-in-class partners around the country to source and stress test strategies and specific investment opportunities.

Customized Turnkey Service

Our technology-enabled turn-key solution makes it easy for our investors to keep an eye on their investments.

Outsourced Real Estate Office

Our turnkey approach means that we act as a trusted advisor forming part of our members' family office or group. We invest alongside our investors in each deal or, if our member prefers, act strictly as an asset manager to maximize member control.

Bespoke Search

We tailor our search service to each member's unique needs and collaborate to develop a customized investment strategy to achieve these goals over a defined time period.

Curated Opportunities

We do not advocate one-size-fits-all investments and only show members what they want, when they want it with a view to helping our members achieve their specific goals.

Flexibility

We can source exceptional opportunities on a deal by deal basis, ensuring maximum control and customization for an investor, or we can acquire a set of assets that meet a prescribed set of parameters established in a programmatic strategy. Alternatively, our members can co-invest alongside other members.

Lifecycle Stewardship

We handle every aspect of the complex real estate ownership lifecycle from origination, property and asset management, ownership structuring, compliance, budgeting and planning and disposition.

Complete Oversight

We seamlessly manage and integrate all the various experts involved in a direct real estate transaction, including attorneys, accountants, and consultants.

Technology-Enabled Platform

Our technology-enabled platform makes it easy for investors to track the performance of their investments and to invest in new opportunities. Our members keep track of their portfolio through our proprietary app and online dashboard. Our cloud based asset management platform ensures that reporting is timely, fast, accurate, and useful.
