Why Empire?

Exceptional private investments,
tailored to select families

Empire Square Group is a private investment firm for a select group of substantial families. We give our members direct access to exceptional opportunities across every asset class, through a customized, turnkey service that achieves outstanding returns. Over the past five years we have earned our members an average 10% annual cash yield and a 21% IRR.

Whole-Portfolio Investing Superior Performance Turnkey Service

01

Whole-Portfolio Investing

We give our member families direct access to exceptional opportunities across every asset class, so they keep maximum control over their wealth and avoid unnecessary layers of cost.

Greater Control

Direct ownership, separately managed accounts and co-investments give families far more control than pooled funds and off-the-shelf products. We tailor the level of control to each family, from full ownership of an asset or business to minority positions alongside trusted partners.

True
Diversification

We invest across public equities, private equity, private credit, real estate, infrastructure and other real assets, and selected alternative strategies. Each family’s portfolio is built as a whole, so that every holding has a clear purpose and the parts work together.

Lower Costs

Investing directly reduces fees and tax leakage. We cut out the middlemen and the stacked fees found in funds of funds, retail products and layered investment structures, and we use the scale of our member families to negotiate better terms wherever outside managers are used.

Multigenerational
Planning

Wealth is built over decades and handed down through generations. We work alongside each family’s trustees and estate advisors so that ownership structures, liquidity and investment strategy support a smooth transfer of wealth to the next generation.

02

Superior Performance

Our proprietary, value-oriented approach is applied with the same discipline across every asset class. Over the last five years it has delivered our members a 10% annual cash yield and a 21% IRR across more than 28 investments totaling over $500 million in value.

Quality Assets

We favour durable, high-quality assets that we expect to outperform over the long run: resilient businesses with strong management, well-located real estate in growing markets, credit backed by real cash flows, and managers with proven, repeatable edges.

Value Focus

We use a fundamental analysis framework for every investment and are relentlessly value-oriented. Our data-driven process draws on a multitude of sources to identify sectors, markets, companies and assets that are poised to outperform, and to exploit macroeconomic and microeconomic dislocations to buy below intrinsic value, as assessed by comparable transactions, replacement cost and cash flow generation.

Unique Access

Our access to exceptional opportunities comes from a proprietary network of long-standing relationships with leading private equity sponsors, founders and operators, fund managers, lenders and real estate owners. We also draw on the Empire Square Group network of member families, and we review a very large number of opportunities to show members only the most compelling for their profile.

Preferential
Financing

Our members enjoy access to financing on preferential terms, from acquisition debt to portfolio-level credit lines. Key relationships with leading banks and lending institutions let us optimise cash yield and liquidity on terms simply not available to most individual investors.

Risk Discipline

Protecting capital comes first. We build every portfolio around each family’s liquidity needs and risk tolerance, stress test positions against a range of scenarios, and monitor concentration, leverage and correlation across the whole portfolio, not just deal by deal.

Seasoned Team

Our investment team, based in New York, brings years of experience and a rigorous institutional approach to sourcing, diligence, analysis and execution in every asset class. We complement it with a select group of best-in-class partners and specialist managers who help us source opportunities and stress test our strategies.

03

Customized Turnkey Service

Our technology-enabled, turnkey service makes it simple for each family to oversee its entire portfolio in one place.

Outsourced
Investment Office

We act as a trusted advisor and an extension of each family’s office, providing the capabilities of a full investment team without the cost of building one. We invest alongside our members in each opportunity or, if a family prefers, act strictly as an advisor and manager to maximise their control.

Bespoke Strategy

We work with each family to understand its goals, time horizon, liquidity needs and values, and together set an investment policy and asset allocation designed to achieve those goals over a defined period.

Curated
Opportunities

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all investing. We show members only what they want, when they want it, with a view to helping each family achieve its specific goals.

Flexibility

We can source opportunities on a deal-by-deal basis for maximum control and customization, or manage a mandate that follows a prescribed set of parameters across asset classes. Members can also co-invest alongside other member families.

Lifecycle
Stewardship

We handle every stage of each investment’s life, from sourcing, diligence and structuring through ongoing monitoring, rebalancing, compliance, budgeting and planning to exit.

Complete
Oversight

We seamlessly coordinate all the experts involved in managing a family’s wealth, including attorneys, accountants, tax advisors, trustees, custodians and outside managers.

Technology-
Enabled Platform

Our technology-enabled platform makes it easy for families to see their entire portfolio, across every asset class, and to invest in new opportunities. Members track their holdings through our proprietary app and online dashboard, and our cloud-based reporting is timely, accurate and useful.
